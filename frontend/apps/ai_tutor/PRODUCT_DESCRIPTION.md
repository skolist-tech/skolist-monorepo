# AI Tutor (aitutor.skolist.com)

AI Tutor is Skolist’s **personal learning companion** for a student. The intended job is simple: after we know _where_ someone is stuck, this product should help them understand it — in their own language, at their own pace — without replacing the teacher.

On the public site it is described as personalized guidance. It is part of the same ecosystem as QGen and Assessments: papers find the gaps; the tutor helps close them.

## Who it is for

- **Students** who need targeted help on weak concepts, not another pile of generic videos.
- **Teachers**, indirectly: a tutor that stays aligned with what the class is actually working on, so they do not have to personally re-explain every gap.
- **Parents**, as a place their child can practise with guidance they can trust more than an anonymous chatbot.

## What it is meant to feel like

- “Start learning” — ask about a topic and get an explanation that actually teaches, not a dump of content.
- “My sessions” — come back to earlier conversations and see what was covered.
- “Subjects” — browse organised paths instead of a blank chat box.

Those experiences are **not fully built yet**. What exists today is the shell of the product (sign-in and a welcome dashboard). Treat this file as the product intent; do not invent features in the UI that this description has not earned.

## What it is not

It is not live doubt classes, not a replacement for school, and not a question-paper factory. Teachers still decide what matters; the tutor is a tool for the student’s next step.

## How it fits with the other apps

Landing explains the vision. QGen and Assessments produce evidence of what a student missed. AI Tutor is where that evidence should turn into **guided practice**. When this product grows, it should still feel like Skolist: diagnosis and strategy, not content overload.
