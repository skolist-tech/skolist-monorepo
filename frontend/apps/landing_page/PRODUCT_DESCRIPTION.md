# Landing site (skolist.com)

This is Skolist’s public website — the first place a school, teacher, parent, or curious visitor learns who we are and whether we are a fit.

It is **not** a classroom tool. It does not generate papers, run exams, or tutor a student. It explains the company, invites people in, and points them to products that are live (today, that is mainly QGen).

## Who it is for

- **Schools** looking for a way to see learning gaps and act on them, not just collect scores.
- **Teachers** who want help diagnosing what each student actually needs.
- **Parents** who want a clearer picture than a percentage on a report card.
- **Students**, indirectly — the site talks about the outcomes we want for them, but they do not “use” this site as an app.

## What visitors should take away

Skolist exists because typical school tests show _what went wrong_ (a low mark) without showing _why_ or _how to fix it_. Teachers cannot personally diagnose 30–40 students in every subject. Extra content libraries and live classes do not solve that core problem.

The promise on this site is **strategy-based personalization**: identify weak concepts, give teachers something they can actually do about them, and help schools, parents, and students see real progress.

The site is also honest about what Skolist is **not**: we do not run live classes, and we do not replace teachers. Teachers stay at the centre; we give them diagnosis, strategy, and measurable improvement.

## What the site covers today

- Home: the story, who benefits, how easy it is meant to feel to adopt, and a demo video.
- The product family (QGen, Checker, Strategy, Notifier, AI Tutor) as one **ecosystem** — each piece solves a different job, together they form a learning loop. QGen is the product that is live and highlighted for “get started.”
- Vision: why the company was started.
- Contact / demo: a way for schools to talk to us.
- Login: a door into Skolist accounts for people who already have one.

## How it fits with the other apps

Think of this as the **front door**. QGen, Assessments, and AI Tutor are the rooms behind it. If a change would confuse a first-time visitor about what Skolist stands for, it belongs here — not in those product apps.
