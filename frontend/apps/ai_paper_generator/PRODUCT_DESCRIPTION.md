# QGen (qgen.skolist.com)

QGen is Skolist’s **question paper generator** for teachers. It is the product that is live today: a teacher can sit down, build a paper that matches their syllabus, and get it into a form they can actually use — without fighting formatting or hunting through stale question banks by hand.

The public line is simple: instantly choose from a very large pool of questions, with auto-formatting.

## Who it is for

- **Teachers** who need a test, worksheet, or practice paper that fits their class and topic — and who still want control over every question.
- **Schools** that want consistent, syllabus-aligned papers without each teacher reinventing the process.
- **Students**, in a smaller way: they can sit papers that teachers send them from this product, and teachers can look at how those attempts went.

## What a teacher can do

- Start from **subjects and concepts** they actually teach, not a generic dump of questions.
- Mix question styles (single-correct, multi-correct, subjective, match the following, and similar classroom types).
- **Generate** new questions with AI when the bank is not enough, then **edit**, regenerate, or correct them until they are classroom-ready (including figures and maths).
- Keep a **question bank** they can search and reuse.
- Assemble a **draft paper**, tidy the structure, and preview how it will look when printed or given to students.
- See a light **analysis** of the paper (balance, difficulty, syllabus coverage) so the set is not accidentally all one flavour.
- **Assign** work and review **student attempts** when they use QGen as the place students write the paper.

Generation is a paid/credit-backed capability: the product is meant to be usable day one, but heavy AI use is not unlimited.

## What it is not

QGen is not a full school website, not a live classroom, and not a JEE/NEET exam centre. It is the **create and give papers** tool. Competitive-exam _simulation_ (NTA-style attempt UI) lives in the Assessments product. Personalized tutoring lives in AI Tutor.

## How it fits with the other apps

On the marketing site, QGen is the first working piece of the Skolist loop: you cannot diagnose gaps without good questions. Other products (Checker, Strategy, Notifier, AI Tutor) are meant to sit around that loop later. If you change QGen, you are changing the main thing a teacher does with Skolist today.
