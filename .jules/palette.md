## 2025-05-14 - [PromptBox & Question Card Enhancements]
**Learning:** Character counters and clear buttons provide immediate functional value to prompt inputs, while hover lift effects and button press micro-animations add a layer of "polish" that makes the app feel more responsive and high-quality.
**Action:** Always consider adding character counters (with pluralization) and clear buttons to textareas. Use `active:scale-95` for tactile button feedback and `hover:-translate-y-1` for interactive cards.
