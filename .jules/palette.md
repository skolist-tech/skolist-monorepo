# Palette's Journal - Skolist UX

## 2025-05-15 - Micro-UX Improvements for Question Generation

**Learning:** Subtle micro-interactions like hover lift effects and character counters significantly improve the perceived quality and usability of form-heavy interfaces. Combining feedback (character counts) with convenience (clear buttons) and tactile feedback (active scale) makes the AI generation process feel more responsive and polished.

**Action:** Consistently apply `active:scale-95 transition-transform` to primary action buttons and `hover:-translate-y-1 hover:shadow-lg transition-all` to interactive cards. Always provide character counters and clear buttons for text-heavy inputs.
